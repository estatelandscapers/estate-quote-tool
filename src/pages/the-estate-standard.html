Six commitments

What "built properly" means, specifically.

  1. Clear scope. Clear pricing.

    A cheaper quote becomes an expensive project when works are excluded, underestimated or left unclear. You see what is included, what is excluded and what has been assumed — before construction begins.

    Transparency from quotation to completion.

  2. Precision from the ground up

    Levels, drainage, excavation, compaction, reinforcement and soil preparation decide how a landscape performs years later — not the finish you see on handover day.

    Build it properly, not just beautifully.

  3. Quality without shortcuts

    Straight lines. Correct levels. Clean finishes. Proper preparation. We would rather address something during construction than leave it for you to discover later.

    Quality is part of the process, not inspected in at the end.

  4. Professional project management

    Earthworks, retaining, drainage, concrete, paving, fencing, turf and planting — coordinated by one organised team, so you deal with us rather than managing numerous trades.

    We manage the project so you don't have to manage us.

  5. Compliance & construction confidence

    We treat landscaping as a construction service. Where drawings, engineering, approvals or certification are required, we identify them and bring in the right professionals.

    If something needs doing properly, we plan for it properly.

  6. We stand behind our work

    We review the completed works with you, hand over care and maintenance information, and address any legitimate workmanship issue professionally.

    Proud of the project years later, not just on handover day.

What it means for price

We're rarely the cheapest quote. Here's exactly why.

When quotes for the same yard are thousands apart, the difference is almost never margin — it's what's underneath. Piering on the wall. Drainage behind it. The base under the driveway. Turf underlay. An itemised quote shows every element, so you can see what you're comparing instead of guessing.

If another quote is cheaper, ask for it itemised and put them side by side. Where ours is higher, you'll be able to see the line it's higher on — and decide whether that line matters to you. Usually it's the one you'd otherwise pay for twice.
