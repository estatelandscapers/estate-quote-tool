Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • When should I get a landscaping quote on a new build?

    Before your builder finishes — ideally once the slab is down. Levels, drainage falls and any footings for future structures are far cheaper to resolve while machinery can still access the site.

  • Can you work from my landscape plan?

    Yes, and it's our preferred starting point. If you don't have one, we can design as part of the package so the whole yard is resolved before pricing.

  • What does a full package cost?

    Complete packages generally start from $25,000, and the quote is itemised element by element, so you can see exactly what drives the number and stage the work if you want to.

  • Do you handle knockdown-rebuilds and duplexes?

    Yes — they're a large share of our work, including running two dwellings' landscapes in parallel on duplex sites.
