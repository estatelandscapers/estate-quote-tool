What we do

The work, specifically.

Design for construction

Our designs are drawn to be built — levels, falls, structural needs and access considered from the first sketch, not discovered at pricing.

Working with your designer

Already have a designer? Even better. We price and build from their documentation and keep them in the loop on any site-driven changes.

Design as part of the package

For full packages, design is folded into the process — concept, revision, then an itemised quote against the agreed plan.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Do I need a design before a quote?

    For complex yards, yes — it’s how you avoid paying for the same decision twice. For straightforward scopes we can quote from a site visit and a clear brief.

  • What does the design cover?

    Layout, levels, materials, planting palette and drainage intent — enough that the itemised quote maps line-for-line onto the drawing.

  • Can I build the design in stages?

    Yes, and the design makes staging safe: footings, drainage and levels are planned for the end state so stage two doesn’t demolish stage one.
