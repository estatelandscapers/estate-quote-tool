Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Why does paving sink or rock?

    Almost always the base: too thin, poorly compacted, or missing edge restraint. Fixing it later means lifting the lot, which is why we don't shortcut it.

  • Pavers or concrete?

    Pavers win on looks and repairability; concrete on cost for large plain areas. Many yards use both — the itemised quote makes the trade-off visible.

  • Is there a minimum size?

    Around 100 square metres standalone; smaller areas we pave as part of a full landscape package.

Picturing a paved area?

Photos of the space and a rough size are enough to price from. We’ll bring samples talk at the site visit.
