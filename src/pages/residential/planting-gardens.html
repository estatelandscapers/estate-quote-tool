What we do

The work, specifically.

Structure planting

Hedges, screens and feature trees that give the yard its bones — including advanced stock where you want privacy this year, not in five.

Garden beds

Prepared soil, correct spacing for the mature size, mulch to hold moisture — the difference between a garden and a collection of pots in the ground.

With the hardscape

Planting is the last trade in our sequence, into finished levels and irrigation — so nothing gets trampled by the works that should have come first.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Will you follow my designer’s planting plan?

    Gladly — designer-led projects are a core part of our work. We price from the schedule and flag substitutions only where supply requires them, with your designer’s sign-off.

  • Native or exotic?

    Whichever suits the spot and the maintenance you’ll actually do. Most of our gardens mix hardy natives for structure with exotics where they earn their keep.

  • How big can you plant?

    Up to advanced ex-ground and large container stock, with access assessed at the site visit — big trees need machinery and planning, not enthusiasm.

Garden beds looking bare?

Photos of the space and its sun through the day are enough to start a planting conversation.
