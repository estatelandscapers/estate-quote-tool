What we do

The work, specifically.

Subsoil drainage

Ag lines in gravel behind retaining walls and under wet lawns, wrapped and connected so they keep working after the first season.

Pits, grates & falls

Collection points where water actually gathers, and surface falls graded so it gets there — away from the house, not toward it.

Before the surfaces

Drainage is sequenced before turf, paving and concrete on every package. It's rule two of The Estate Standard.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • My lawn is always soggy — fixable?

    Usually, with subsoil drainage and regraded falls. As a standalone job it depends on scale; as part of re-landscaping the area, always.

  • Do retaining walls need drainage?

    Yes, every one. Water building up behind a wall is the most common reason walls fail — ag line and gravel backfill are non-negotiable in our builds.

  • Can you connect to stormwater?

    Yes, where a lawful connection point exists. Where the work crosses into plumbing territory, licensed plumbers handle that portion.
