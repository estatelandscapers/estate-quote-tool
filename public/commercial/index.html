How we work

The part that decides whether your program holds.

Landscaping is usually the last trade on site, which means it absorbs every delay that came before it and still gets asked to finish on the original date. Four things make that survivable.

  1. Priced off drawings

    We quote from the landscape plan, civil set and levels — not a walk-through and a guess. Variations then come from real scope changes, not from what was missed at tender.

  2. Documented before we start

    SWMS, ITPs, insurances and licence details go in before the first machine arrives, in whatever format your site requires.

  3. Set out and levels first

    Drainage falls, retaining levels and set-out are confirmed before anything is built on top of them. Nearly every expensive landscape rework traces back to this step being rushed.

  4. Handed over with the paperwork

    Engineer certification where walls require it, ITP sign-offs, as-built notes and a defects walk — so practical completion isn't held up by missing documents.

Common questions

What contractors ask us before inviting a submission.

Answered here so you are not chasing us for the basics.

  • Which states do you work in?

    New South Wales and Queensland for commercial landscape packages.

  • Do you provide SWMS, ITPs and site documentation?

    Yes. Safe Work Method Statements, Inspection and Test Plans, insurances and licence details are submitted before works commence, in the format your site requires. Our integrated management system is currently progressing through ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment) and ISO 45001 (safety) certification.

  • What insurance do you carry?

    $20 million public liability and current workers compensation. NSW Fair Trading licence 487076C. Landscape Association membership 41219.

  • Do you undertake civil works?

    Not under Estate Landscapers. Bulk earthworks, subdivision civil, stormwater infrastructure and road works are delivered by our civil arm, Infra Landscapers.

  • Can you price from drawings?

    Yes. We quote from the landscape plan, civil set and levels rather than from a site walk-through, which keeps variations tied to genuine scope changes.

  • Do you cover establishment and maintenance periods?

    Yes. We maintain landscape works through defects liability and establishment periods to final handover.

  • What do you need to prepare a tender submission?

    Landscape drawings, the civil set, any bill of quantities, and the construction program. If the program is tight we will tell you before submitting rather than after award.

  • What kinds of clients do you work with?

    Residential builders and developers, councils and government, strata and body corporate managers, and commercial property owners.
