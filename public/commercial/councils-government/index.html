How we work with you

Built around how your side of the table runs.

Compliant by construction

SWMS, ITPs and hold-point inspections built into the program, evidenced as we go — not reconstructed at completion. Our integrated management system is progressing through ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certification.

Priced from the documents

Specification, drawings and schedules priced as issued, with departures declared at tender — so evaluation compares like with like.

Through to PC and beyond

Establishment and maintenance periods delivered as specified, with records that make handover to your parks team clean.

Common questions

Asked before most engagements.

  • Are you set up for government WHS requirements?

    Yes — SWMS and site-specific safety documentation are standard on every commercial job, and our integrated management system is progressing through ISO 45001, 9001 and 14001 certification.

  • Can you meet insurance and licensing thresholds?

    $20M public liability, current workers compensation, NSW Fair Trading licence 487076C, Landscape Association member 41219 — certificates supplied with every submission.

  • Do you deliver maintenance periods?

    Yes, priced as a line item and reported through the defects and establishment period to final completion.
