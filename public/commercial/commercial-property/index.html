Common questions

Asked before most engagements.

  • Can you work around trading hours?

    Yes — staging, containment and out-of-hours components are priced into the program where the asset needs them.

  • Do you coordinate with our facility manager?

    One point of contact on our side, aligned with your FM’s induction, access and reporting requirements.

  • What documentation do you provide?

    SWMS and insurances before we start, progress records through the works, and handover documentation including care requirements for whoever maintains the asset after us.
