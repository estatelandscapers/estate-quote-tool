What we do

The work, specifically.

The structural half

Earthworks, retaining walls, drainage, concrete and footings — the parts that decide whether everything above them lasts.

The visible half

Paving, fencing, decking, turf and planting — installed on prepared ground, not instead of preparing it.

The order of operations

Set-out and levels first, drainage before surfaces, softworks last. That sequence is The Estate Standard, and it's on every quote we send.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Do you take on single elements, or only full packages?

    Both. Retaining walls from about 20 linear metres and turf, paving or garden areas from about 100 square metres stand alone; smaller elements we build inside complete packages, which generally start from $25,000.

  • Who manages the trades?

    We do — the core trades are our own crews, with engineers and certifiers brought in where the work requires them. One point of contact for the whole job.

  • How do I compare quotes fairly?

    Ask every contractor for an itemised quote and put them side by side. Differences are almost always in what's underneath — base, drainage, piering — not in margin.
