What we do

The work, specifically.

Designed with the planting

Drip to beds, spray or drip to turf, zones matched to what each area actually needs — decided alongside the planting plan, not after it.

Installed before surfaces

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Do you repair existing irrigation systems?

    No — repairs to existing systems are outside what our crews do, and we’ll refer you to a specialist who does them properly. We install new systems as part of landscape projects.

  • Is irrigation worth it in Sydney?

    For new turf and planting, establishment watering decides success — a zoned system does it consistently while you’re busy. After establishment, it protects the investment through dry spells within water rules.

  • Tank, mains or both?

    Whatever the site offers — systems can draw from tanks with mains backup where plumbing allows, configured by the licensed trades for that portion.
