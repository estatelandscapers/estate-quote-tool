What we do

The work, specifically.

Decking

Hardwood and composite decks on properly set posts and bearers, with ventilation and clearances that keep timber honest for years.

Pergolas & shade

Posted structures sized for their spans and anchored to real footings — planned into the slab and landscape rather than bolted on after.

Stairs & platforms

Level changes handled with consistent risers and solid stringers, tied into retaining and paving so the whole transition works as one.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Do structures need approval?

    Many do, depending on size, height and boundary distance — NSW exempt-development rules cover some smaller structures. We flag what your build needs as part of quoting rather than after.

  • Hardwood or composite decking?

    Hardwood for the real thing with maintenance; composite for near-zero upkeep at a higher upfront cost. The itemised quote shows both if you’re deciding.

  • Can you add a structure to an existing yard?

    Yes, though access and protection of finished work set the approach. Cheapest time is always while the landscape is being built — that’s why we plan footings early.
